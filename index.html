Privacy Policy

The Short Version

✓ All your data stays on your device — we never upload it anywhere.
✓ We collect no personal information: no name, no email, no account required.

1. Who We Are

LiftLog is a mobile application built to help individuals log gym workouts, track progress, and get workout recommendations. LiftLog is operated by the LiftLog team ("we", "us", or "our").

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us at logthelift@gmail.com.

2. What Data We Store

LiftLog stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Workout logs — dates, exercises, sets, reps, weights, durations, and notes you enter.
  • Custom exercises — any exercises you create with their name, muscle group, equipment, and type.
  • Feature suggestions — any feedback you choose to write inside the app.
  • App preferences — your chosen colour theme and muscle group order.

This data is stored using AsyncStorage, a standard local storage mechanism provided by React Native. It is never transmitted to any server, cloud service, or third party.

3. What Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any other identifying information.
  • Your location or GPS data.
  • Your contacts, calendar, or camera (unless you explicitly use a share feature).
  • Health or biometric data beyond what you manually enter.
  • Browsing history or usage patterns.
  • Advertising identifiers.

LiftLog does not require you to create an account or sign in.

4. Crash Reporting (Sentry)

To help us identify and fix bugs, LiftLog uses Sentry, a third-party crash reporting service. When the app crashes or encounters an unexpected error in a production build, an anonymous crash report is sent to Sentry containing:

  • The type of error and the line of code where it occurred.
  • Your device type and operating system version (e.g. "iPhone 15, iOS 17.4").
  • The app version number.

Crash reports do not contain any workout data, notes, or personal information. Sentry's privacy policy is available at sentry.io/privacy.

Crash reporting is disabled while the app is in development and only activates in production App Store / Google Play builds.

5. Data Security

All data stored by LiftLog is protected by your device's built-in security:

  • On iOS, app data is stored in an isolated sandboxed container protected by the iOS data protection model.
  • On Android, app data is stored in internal storage, accessible only to LiftLog and protected by Android's app isolation model.

Unique identifiers generated within the app (for workouts, exercises, and suggestions) use cryptographically secure random number generation (expo-crypto) to prevent ID guessing.

6. Your Rights & Data Control

You are in full control of your data at all times. From the Stats tab in the app you can:

  • Export your data — generates a complete JSON backup of all your workouts, custom exercises, and suggestions, which you can save or share.
  • Delete all data — permanently removes all stored data from your device. This action cannot be undone.

Uninstalling the app from your device will also permanently remove all locally stored data.

7. Children's Privacy

LiftLog is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will take steps to remove it.

8. Third-Party Services

LiftLog uses the following third-party libraries and services:

  • Sentry — anonymous crash reporting (production only). Privacy Policy.
  • Expo — the development framework used to build the app. Privacy Policy.

No advertising networks, analytics platforms, or data brokers are used.
